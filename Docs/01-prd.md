# Product Requirement Document

## Project Name
E-commerce Platform with Inventory Management

## Overview
Build a smart e-commerce system that supports online selling, real-time inventory management, and intelligent stock control to reduce overselling, stockouts, and manual operations.

## Business Goals
- Enable businesses to sell products online efficiently
- Prevent overselling and inventory inconsistencies
- Reduce manual inventory tracking
- Support future expansion (multi-warehouse, AI forecasting)

## Scope
### In Scope
Customer: 
- Browse products
- View availability
- Place orders and make payments
- Track order status
Admin / Store Manager:
- Manage products and inventory
- Monitor stock levels
- Handle orders and cancellations
- View reports and alerts
System (Automation):
- Auto-update inventory
- Trigger low-stock alerts
- Lock stock during checkout
### Out of Scope
- Multi-vendor marketplace
- Multi-warehouse inventory
- Dropshipping support
- Advanced promotions and coupons
- AI-based demand forecasting
- Native mobile applications
